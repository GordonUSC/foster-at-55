For Foster · FANGSPEARE · Lemurian script studies

There is no font of Lemuria. So we drew one.

Lemuria began as a zoologist's guess in 1864 and became a myth of an ancient, advanced people. No script of it survives, because none was ever found. In the same 19th-century myth, Hyperborea, your sage's home, came just before Lemuria in the chain of lost lands. That leaves the alphabet open. Three studies, each built from your brief: rune bones, alien engineering, dubstep weight, and HAMDI's ring.

A · Rune Circuit

Rune bones drawn as one engineered line, every stroke end capped with a node like a circuit pad, and HAMDI's ring orbiting the F. Ancient letters, alien wiring.
